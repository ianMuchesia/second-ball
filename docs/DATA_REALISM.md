## How it works

- **Simulated:** 500 free-simulation matches (seeds 10000–10499),
  every ordered pair of the league's clubs in turn, dry weather. Same seeds =
  same numbers.
- **Reference:** season 2024/25, top five European leagues, downloaded
  by `evals/realism/fetch_reference.py` into `evals/realism/reference.yaml`
  (source URL and access date per number). band = [lowest, highest] of the five league values.
  No reference number is typed from memory.
- **Pass rule:** the simulated value's 95% interval (bootstrap over matches,
  2000 resamples) lies inside the reference band.
- **Tuning:** engine knobs were tuned against these seeds (see DECISIONS.md).
  A hold-out run on seeds never used for tuning guards against over-fitting:
  seeds 50000–50499: 6 of 8 compared metrics pass (not passing: goals_per_match, red_cards_per_match).

## Definitions and known gaps

- **Shots on target** = goals + shots the keeper saves.
- **xG** is the engine's own model (`engine/config/engine.yaml`); the reference is
  Understat's model. Same idea, different models.
- **PPDA** = opponent open-play passes in their own 60% of the pitch, divided by
  tackles, interceptions and fouls in that zone. Understat does not publish its
  exact zone; the standard definition is assumed (see reference.yaml).
- **Possession** = share of passes (FBref's method). **Possession spread** = the
  standard deviation of the clubs' average possession. No current free source.
- **Pass completion / passes per match:** no current free source (FBref removed
  its advanced stats in January 2026 and blocks automated access).
- Weather is dry in every simulated match; real seasons have mixed weather.
- **Known gap: club spread.** League averages pass, but clubs differ in the
  wrong way: long-ball clubs (e.g. Ironvale, Port Ilvane) take far too few
  shots and the possession range is too narrow (see the per-club table).
  Club-level spread is not yet compared against a reference.
